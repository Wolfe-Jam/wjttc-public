# WJTTC 🍊 Brand Story

## The Big Orange Heritage

### Origins: 1968

The year 1968 was pivotal. Bruce McLaren's racing team unveiled their iconic **Papaya Orange** livery at the Can-Am series - a bold departure from the traditional British Racing Green. That same year, wolfejam was born.

His first car? Orange, of course.

### McLaren: The Testing Company

Most people know McLaren as a racing team. Insiders know them as a **testing company** that happens to race.

> *"When brakes must work flawlessly, so must our code"*

McLaren's obsession with telemetry, component validation, and relentless testing isn't about winning races - it's about **not dying**. When you're hitting 200mph and need to brake for a hairpin, "mostly works" isn't acceptable.

This philosophy - championship-grade testing where failure is not an option - is the DNA of WJTTC.

### Why is Big Orange a Badge?

In F1, the best teams don't just win — they set the standard. Big Orange is a badge, not a score. The maximum score is 100%.

**Big Orange (🍊)** = 100% pass rate + awarded for excellence beyond metrics

Like a Michelin Star, it recognises you've gone **above and beyond** — not just passed all tests, but demonstrated the kind of professional polish that separates good from exceptional.

## The WJTTC Story

### IANA Registration

Before WJTTC, wolfejam created the `.faf` format - **Foundational AI-context Format**. It became the first and only MCP-related format to achieve [IANA registration](https://www.iana.org/assignments/media-types/application/vnd.faf+yaml) (`application/vnd.faf+yaml`).

How? Obsessive attention to technical detail. Comprehensive testing. Documentation that anticipated every question.

The same approach that earned IANA approval now grades MCP servers.
